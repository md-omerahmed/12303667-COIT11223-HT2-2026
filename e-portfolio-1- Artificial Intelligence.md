# Artefact 1: What is Artifical Intelligence (AI)?

# Summary

Learn Bright YouTube channel gives an explanation of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in simple and easy terms. It presents the concepts of Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI), Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI), and demonstrates the existing applications of AI in daily life, such as voice assistants, recommendation systems, robotics and automation. The video also focuses on the advantages of AI, like increasing efficiency and improving problem-solving, as well as issues like job replacement and the improper usage of technology.

# Reflection and Justification

I chose this video as it was an excellent introduction to the concept of AI prior to moving on to more in-depth ethical considerations in the unit. Prior to watching it, I'd been mostly using AI in relation to ChatGPT, but I learned that there are many years of AI in various forms. This artefact is directly related to our unit as the concepts of AI are fundamental to the discussion of governance, privacy, fairness, and ethical responsibilities of an ICT professional. It allowed me to gain a better understanding that AI is more than generative AI.

# Artefact 2 – News Article (AI in Healthcare)
<img width="1136" height="939" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/da7fb34a-7b57-40c6-9205-193b31296ca2" />
https://abcnews.com/Health/ai-model-helps-patients-diagnoses-after-years-uncertainty/story?id=134040827

# Summary

The article discusses the growing application of AI in healthcare, specifically in the analysis of medical images, predictions, and patient care, and how hospitals are benefiting from these advancements. AI systems can analyze thousands of medical records in a matter of seconds, facilitating quicker diagnoses and clinical decision-making. Meanwhile, the article addresses issues of data privacy, transparency and algorithmic bias, pointing out the need for human oversight.

# Reflection and Justification

This article was selected for the fact that healthcare is a socio-beneficial application of AI, but also a space with potentially high ethical implications when systems are incorrect or have bias. I learnt that the use of AI should augment and augment human judgment. It links with our workshop on responsible AI, accountability and fairness. As an aspiring ICT professional, I discovered that developers have a responsibility to ensure that the systems produced are transparent, reliable and do not harm patients.

# Artefact 3: Scholary Article
<img width="925" height="820" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3db363c-73be-431d-b0c4-19d6a3db36de" />
https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-019-0088-2?utm_source=chatgpt.com

# Summary

The scholarly article addresses the issue of fairness in AI systems and covers how AI models can inadvertently discriminate against specific groups when trained with biased data. The authors suggest enhancing data quality, ensuring transparency, and periodically auditing AI systems to minimize inequities. The paper shows that technical performance is insufficient to develop AI.

# Reflection and Justification

This article was chosen for this peer-reviewed piece of research as it was able to provide evidence for many of the ethical concerns that were raised during the workshop. I learned from academic writing that this is ethics behind AI and not only about technology and it's mission to protect people's rights and give them equal treatment. This artefact further strengthened my beliefs that ICT professionals, apart from coding, also have ethical duties. Fairness, accountability and social impact must be considered throughout the system development lifecycle to ensure Responsible AI.
